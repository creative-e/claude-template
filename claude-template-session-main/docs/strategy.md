## Product

Google Docs — a browser-based collaborative document editor. Real-time multi-user editing, comments and suggestions, version history, and deep integration with Drive, Gmail, and the broader Google Workspace suite. Works offline, on web and mobile, with AI-assisted writing (Help me write, summaries) built in.

## Market segments

- **Knowledge workers in SMBs and enterprises** on Google Workspace — primary daily-use segment.
- **Education (K-12 and higher ed)** via Google Workspace for Education — students, teachers, administrators.
- **Individual / prosumer users** on free Google accounts — writers, freelancers, personal use.
- **Cross-functional teams collaborating with external partners** — anyone who needs to share a link and co-edit without friction.

## Value proposition

**Frictionless real-time collaboration in the browser, with your documents available anywhere and your collaborators one link away.**

- **Collaboration-first**: multiple people editing the same doc live, with presence, comments, and suggestion mode — no merge conflicts, no "final_v3_FINAL.docx".
- **Zero install, zero sync setup**: works in any modern browser; autosaves to Drive; available on any device signed into the account.
- **Sharing by link**: granular permissions (view / comment / edit), domain-scoped or public, revocable at any time.
- **Workspace integration**: native handoff with Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Sheets, Slides, and Drive search.
- **AI assistance built in**: drafting, rewriting, summarizing, and proofreading inside the editor.
- **Free tier + predictable Workspace pricing**: low barrier for individuals; per-seat plans for organizations.

## Primary competitors

Microsoft Word (Microsoft 365), Notion, Dropbox Paper, Apple Pages, Coda.

## What we are *not* optimizing for

- Heavyweight desktop-class layout and typesetting (that's Word / InDesign territory).
- Structured wiki / database hybrids (that's Notion / Coda).
- Offline-first single-user writing (that's iA Writer, Ulysses, etc.).
